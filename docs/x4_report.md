## Question

TRACE (CIKM '26) section 7.1 leaves "separate tracks with independent budgets" as future work: greedy selection cuts fraud but serves fewer honest providers, and in the partitioned view (each buyer sees only its own 60 jobs) greedy is the riskier choice. X4 asks whether TRACE+EB(B), the budgeted exploration track the wallet enforces on-chain (D3), moves the fraud vs served-honest frontier beyond the paper's own policies.

## What ran

- **Fork.** Public Trace_CIKM @94c4b9f vendored in `sim/` with no vendored file modified; three missing modules stubbed as no-ops; every addition listed in `sim/patches.json`.
- **Repro gate (M6, X4 step 0).** TRACE-no-bandit, strategic-default, centralized Table 1 contract, seeds 42-51: mean fraud 3.4 [r:sim/results/repro_table1.json#mean] sats, sd 3.86 [r:sim/results/repro_table1.json#sd], against the paper's 4.7 [r:sim/results/repro_table1.json#paperMean] +/- 4.3 [r:sim/results/repro_table1.json#band]; pass = yes [r:sim/results/repro_table1.json#pass].
- **Regime.** Partitioned, the author's `_partitionedViewWorker` contract behind Table 9: 5 independent buyers, 60 jobs each (60 rounds x 1 job), N = 1,000 agents, 30% malicious, propagation delay 2. Each buyer session runs in its own process: the author's single-process worker carries module state from one buyer into the next, which contradicts the paper's "no shared state between buyers".
- **Policies.** TRACE+EB(B) for B in {0, 1, 2, 5, 10}%, plus the baselines TRACE-full, TRACE-no-bandit, REPUTATION and EigenTrust rerun in the same fork and seeds. Attacks: strategic-default, collusion-ring, sybil-cluster, game-theoretic (p75). Seeds 42-51. Cells: 360 [r:results/x4_h1.json#nCells].
- **TRACE+EB(B)** (`sim/hx/ebPolicy.ts`): per job, if exploration spend + price <= B x total spend (this job included), pick by TRACE's Thompson-sampling selection among eligible unproven, non-flagged providers; otherwise pick by TRACE-no-bandit's greedy selection among proven providers, or among all non-flagged ones while none is proven. Flagged providers are never picked. Tier rule (`sim/hx/tierRule.ts`): flagged = the router's existing Sybil filters fire; proven = not flagged and the 95% LCB under Beta(1,3) is at least that of 3 successes and 0 failures; everything else is unproven.
- **Metrics.** Fraud = sats paid to malicious providers on jobs that defaulted, summed over the 5 buyers. Served-honest = distinct honest providers given >= 1 job, summed over the 5 buyer sessions (each session draws its own market of 700 honest providers). Malicious-routing rate MR = share of jobs routed to malicious providers.
- **Test.** For each attack, the 10-seed mean points of the four baselines span a convex hull. EB(B) moves the frontier iff no convex combination has fraud <= and served-honest >= the EB point (exact LP via the 2-D segment test, cross-checked on a simplex grid), in >= 95% of 1,000 paired seed-bootstrap resamples (RNG seed 20261024).

## Reading the result

- strategic-default: EB(5%) puts 4.8% [r:results/x4_h1.json#perRegimeAttack.0.exploreSpendShareAtBstar] of its spend into the exploration track; its point is dominated outright by TRACE-full, REPUTATION, EIGENTRUST.
- collusion-ring: EB(5%) puts 4.8% [r:results/x4_h1.json#perRegimeAttack.1.exploreSpendShareAtBstar] of its spend into the exploration track; its point is dominated outright by TRACE-full, REPUTATION.
- sybil-cluster: EB(5%) puts 4.8% [r:results/x4_h1.json#perRegimeAttack.2.exploreSpendShareAtBstar] of its spend into the exploration track; its point is dominated outright by TRACE-full, REPUTATION.
- game-theoretic: EB(5%) puts 4.8% [r:results/x4_h1.json#perRegimeAttack.3.exploreSpendShareAtBstar] of its spend into the exploration track; its point is dominated outright by EIGENTRUST.
- The budget binds as designed (the exploration share sits just under B), so EB(B) is a bounded track. Its points still fall inside the baselines' hull on every attack: in the partitioned view the baselines that serve the most honest providers do so at comparable or lower fraud. That is consistent with the paper's section 7.3 observation that with thin local evidence exploration is insurance rather than waste; restricting the non-exploration track to proven payees costs openness here without buying lower fraud.

## Engineering notes

- **DB backend.** A CPU profile put most simulation time in the Prisma 7 runtime, so the sweep runs on an in-memory implementation of the Prisma subset the simulator uses (`sim/hx/memPrisma.ts`), installed through the `globalThis.prisma` hook `src/lib/db.ts` already has. Gate (`sim/hx/memCheck.ts`): 48 [r:sim/results/mem_check.json#deterministicUnitsIdentical] of 48 [r:sim/results/mem_check.json#deterministicUnitsCompared] deterministic Prisma+SQLite session units reproduce bit for bit (job-sequence hashes, all four attacks), and 10 [r:sim/results/mem_check.json#reproCellsIdentical] of 10 [r:sim/results/mem_check.json#reproCellsCompared] repro cells give identical fraud, served-honest and MR; gate pass = yes [r:sim/results/mem_check.json#pass]. REPUTATION is the exception by construction: its seeded jitter stream is also drawn by Prisma's id generator, so all REPUTATION cells use the memory backend.
- **Runtime.** 1800 [r:sim/results/x4_timing.json#partitionedSweep.sessionUnits] session units: 1752 [r:sim/results/x4_timing.json#partitionedSweep.byBackend.memory.units] on the memory backend at a mean of 3.3 [r:sim/results/x4_timing.json#partitionedSweep.byBackend.memory.meanSec] s and 0.23 [r:sim/results/x4_timing.json#partitionedSweep.byBackend.memory.meanRssGB] GB each, 48 [r:sim/results/x4_timing.json#partitionedSweep.byBackend.prisma-sqlite.units] on Prisma+SQLite at 27.8 [r:sim/results/x4_timing.json#partitionedSweep.byBackend.prisma-sqlite.meanSec] s and 0.83 [r:sim/results/x4_timing.json#partitionedSweep.byBackend.prisma-sqlite.meanRssGB] GB each; the final run took 2136 [r:sim/results/x4_timing.json#partitionedSweep.lastRun.wallClockSec] s at up to 6 [r:sim/results/x4_timing.json#partitionedSweep.lastRun.maxObservedConcurrent] parallel units (`sim/results/x4_timing.json`).
- **Determinism.** The vendored REPUTATION/PRICE utilities draw unseeded `Math.random()` jitter; the worker seeds it per buyer session, so every cell reproduces from its seed.
- **Implementation choices** fixed before the scored runs are logged in `DECISIONS.md` ([SIM] lines).

## Limits

- Simulated market, fixed-script adversaries and binary outcomes, as in the paper; the sparse-centralized regime was cut.
- Served-honest sums distinct providers over 5 independently drawn buyer markets, so its scale differs from the paper's centralized Table 8 (x/700).
- X4 informs the wallet's default B; the wallet's own guarantee is the hard per-window cap (X5), which does not depend on this result.
